\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}
% =============================================================================

BitcoinPurple (BTCP) is a decentralised peer-to-peer digital currency designed
to enable fast, low-cost, censorship-resistant transactions without reliance on
intermediaries or central authorities.  Derived directly from the Bitcoin Core
codebase, BitcoinPurple retains Bitcoin's battle-tested SHA-256d proof-of-work
consensus, its UTXO model, and its full suite of modern script capabilities
(SegWit, Taproot), while introducing three key refinements:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{1-minute block interval} — ten times faster confirmations than
        Bitcoin.
  \item \textbf{120-block difficulty retarget} (\(\approx\)2\,h) — rapid
        hashrate-responsive adjustment with a 4$\times$ change ceiling per epoch.
  \item \textbf{Strict monetary cap of 1\,000\,000 BTCP} — all soft-forks
        (including Taproot) active from genesis; Lightning Network support built
        in from the start.
\end{itemize}

BitcoinPurple launched with no premine on 4 August 2023.  The initial block
reward is 1 BTCP, halving every 500\,000 blocks, yielding an asymptotic supply
of \(\approx\)1\,000\,000 BTCP.  Infrastructure development is ongoing, with a
dedicated Electrum SPV wallet and a Lightning Network node implementation on the
near-term roadmap.

\section{Introduction}
% =============================================================================

Since the publication of the Bitcoin whitepaper in 2008, blockchain-based
digital currencies have established that peer-to-peer electronic cash is not
only theoretically sound but practically deployable at scale.  Bitcoin remains
the gold standard for security and decentralisation; however, its 10-minute
block interval and comparatively slow difficulty adjustment make it sub-optimal
for everyday micropayments and high-frequency retail use.

BitcoinPurple addresses this gap without sacrificing Bitcoin's proven security
model.  By shortening the block interval to 60 seconds and tightening the
difficulty-adjustment window to 120 blocks (\(\approx\)2 hours), the network
achieves a user experience comparable to traditional payment processors while
remaining fully permissionless and censorship-resistant.

The project was founded by \textbf{Alym Wehrli} and launched with a public
genesis block on \textbf{4 August 2023}.  It is currently maintained and
developed by \textbf{Davide Grilli} and \textbf{Maurito83}.  The codebase
is a fork of Bitcoin Core, meaning that security improvements and protocol
upgrades from the upstream Bitcoin ecosystem can be integrated with minimal
friction.  All modern Bitcoin soft-forks — SegWit, CSV, CLTV, and Taproot — are
active from block 0, providing a clean slate for second-layer development.

\subsection{Design Principles}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{Bitcoin-compatible security} — SHA-256d PoW means BTCP is
        mineable with the same ASIC hardware as Bitcoin, lowering the barrier
        to participation.
  \item \textbf{Speed} — 1-minute blocks reduce median confirmation latency
        from \(\approx\)5 minutes (Bitcoin) to \(\approx\)30 seconds.
  \item \textbf{Scarcity} — a hard cap of 1\,000\,000 BTCP mirrors Bitcoin's
        deflationary model at a more accessible nominal unit price.
  \item \textbf{Fairness} — no premine; distribution begins from the genesis
        coinbase.
  \item \textbf{Futureproofing} — native Taproot and Lightning support enable
        privacy, smart contracts, and instant off-chain payments from day one.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Proof-of-Work Algorithm}

BitcoinPurple uses \textbf{SHA-256d} (double SHA-256), identical to Bitcoin.
This choice was deliberate:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Existing SHA-256 ASIC miners can mine BTCP without hardware
        modification.
  \item The algorithm has been subject to over a decade of cryptanalysis
        with no known weaknesses at the current security margin.
  \item Mining pools and firmware developed for Bitcoin are compatible after
        a simple RPC endpoint change.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Difficulty Adjustment}

BTCP retargets difficulty at every block height $H$ where $H \bmod 120 = 0$.
The algorithm is identical to Bitcoin's, but operating on a 120-block window
rather than 2016 blocks:

\[
  T_{\text{new}} = T_{\text{old}}
    \times \frac{t_{\text{clamped}}}{7200},
  \qquad
  t_{\text{clamped}} = \mathrm{clamp}(\text{actual\_timespan},\;1800,\;28800)\,\text{s}
\]

\[
  T_{\text{new}} = \min\!\left(T_{\text{new}},\; T_{\text{limit}}\right)
\]

This design means:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Blocks produced faster than 60 s increase difficulty.
  \item Blocks produced slower than 60 s decrease difficulty.
  \item A single epoch can never move difficulty by more than a factor of 4.
\end{itemize}

The short retarget window provides rapid response to large hashrate changes
— a critical property for a young network where mining participation may be
volatile.

\subsection{Block-Time-Scaled Timeout Parameters}

\textbf{This is the most safety-critical aspect of LN on BTCP.}
Because BTCP has 1-minute blocks (vs Bitcoin's 10-minute blocks), all
block-count-based LN timeouts \emph{must} be scaled by a factor of \(\times 10\)
to maintain the same real-world security windows:

\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\caption{Recommended LN timeout parameters for BTCP}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrl@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Bitcoin} & \textbf{BTCP} & \textbf{Wall-clock} \\
\midrule
\texttt{to\_self\_delay} (justice window)         & 144 blocks & \textbf{1\,440 blocks} & $\approx$ 24 h \\
\texttt{cltv\_expiry\_delta} per hop              &  40 blocks & \textbf{400 blocks}   & $\approx$ 6.7 h \\
\texttt{min\_final\_cltv\_expiry\_delta}          &   9 blocks & \textbf{90 blocks}    & $\approx$ 90 min \\
Max remote \texttt{to\_self\_delay}               & 2\,016 blocks & \textbf{20\,160 blocks} & $\approx$ 14 days \\
\texttt{max\_cltv\_expiry}                        & 2\,016 blocks & \textbf{20\,160 blocks} & $\approx$ 14 days \\
Channel funding confirmation target               & 3–6 blocks & \textbf{30–60 blocks} & $\approx$ 30–60 min \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Failure to scale these parameters would leave channel operators with a
dangerously short penalty window — for example, Bitcoin's default
\texttt{to\_self\_delay} of 144 blocks would grant only 2.4 hours to react
to a cheating peer on BTCP, instead of the intended 24 hours.

\section{Use Cases}
% =============================================================================

BitcoinPurple's combination of fast block times, low fees, and strong security
makes it suitable for a broad range of payment scenarios:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{Retail payments} — 1-minute confirmations enable in-store
        payments where the customer can leave after a single on-chain
        confirmation.

  \item \textbf{Remittances} — censorship-resistant international transfers
        with no correspondent-bank fees and near-Bitcoin security guarantees.

  \item \textbf{Micropayments} — the low nominal unit price and
        Lightning-ready infrastructure support sub-cent micropayments for
        content, APIs, and IoT applications.

  \item \textbf{Online purchases} — faster confirmation reduces cart-abandonment
        risk for e-commerce merchants compared to 10-minute-block chains.

  \item \textbf{Cross-border transactions} — permissionless global reach with
        no restrictions based on jurisdiction, identity, or transaction size.

  \item \textbf{Lightning Network instant payments (roadmap)} — once the LN
        layer is live, payments will settle in milliseconds with fees
        measured in fractions of a satoshi, enabling real-time streaming
        payments and machine-to-machine commerce.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Governance Model}

BitcoinPurple follows a community-driven governance model in the spirit of
open-source Bitcoin development.  Protocol changes are discussed openly in the
community Telegram group
(\href{https://t.me/+o3I0A1q1W29mMmQ0}{t.me/+o3I0A1q1W29mMmQ0}) and, once
community consensus is reached, are implemented by the core developers and
released under the project's open-source licence.

Key principles:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Transparency} — all code changes are published publicly on
        GitHub before activation.
  \item \textbf{Community input} — major protocol decisions are discussed
        in the Telegram community before any implementation begins.
  \item \textbf{Miner signalling} — soft-fork activations require 90\%
        (108\,/\,120 blocks) miner signalling within the retarget window.
  \item \textbf{No premine / no ICO} — there are no founders' tokens,
        investor allocations, or pre-sold coins; every BTCP in existence was
        earned through mining.
\end{itemize}

\section{Security Considerations}
% =============================================================================

\subsection{51\% Attack Resistance}

As a young SHA-256d network, BitcoinPurple shares its proof-of-work algorithm
with Bitcoin.  This is a deliberate security trade-off: ASIC hardware developed
for Bitcoin can also mine BTCP, which expands the accessible miner base.
However, as hashrate grows, the cost of a 51\% attack increases proportionally.
The project encourages mining participation to strengthen network security.

\subsection{Short Block Interval and Orphan Rate}

1-minute blocks produce a higher orphan rate than 10-minute blocks if network
propagation latency is non-negligible relative to the block interval.  Compact
block relay (BIP152, inherited from Bitcoin Core) mitigates this by propagating
only missing transactions after a block header is announced, reducing propagation
time to well under one second on modern infrastructure.

\subsection{Lightning Network Safety}

As documented in Section~5.3, LN timeout parameters \emph{must} be scaled for
BTCP's 1-minute block cadence.  Using unmodified Bitcoin LN defaults on BTCP
would reduce the justice window from 24 hours to 2.4 hours — an unacceptable
security degradation.  The official BTCP LN fork will ship with correct scaled
defaults.

\subsection{Coinbase Maturity}

Mining rewards require 100 blocks (\(\approx\)100 minutes) before they can be
spent.  This provides protection against chain reorganisations invalidating
recently mined rewards.

\section{Conclusion}
% =============================================================================

BitcoinPurple represents a carefully considered evolution of the Bitcoin
protocol, optimised for the everyday payment use case without sacrificing the
core properties — decentralisation, censorship-resistance, and verifiable
scarcity — that give Bitcoin its value.

The key design decisions are mutually reinforcing:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item SHA-256d PoW preserves compatibility with the world's largest pool of
        dedicated mining hardware.
  \item 1-minute blocks dramatically reduce confirmation latency for end users.
  \item 120-block difficulty retarget keeps the network stable under volatile
        hashrate without compromising security.
  \item A hard cap of 1\,000\,000 BTCP, with no premine, ensures that no single
        party holds a structural advantage.
  \item Native Taproot and Lightning readiness from genesis position BTCP for
        a second-layer ecosystem without requiring a contentious soft-fork
        campaign later.
\end{itemize}

With the full-node software stable, an ElectrumX indexing layer deployed, and
an Electrum SPV wallet and Lightning Network implementation actively in
development, BitcoinPurple is progressing toward its vision of a fast,
accessible, and secure global payment network.

The community is invited to participate in development discussions on Telegram
(\href{https://t.me/+o3I0A1q1W29mMmQ0}{t.me/+o3I0A1q1W29mMmQ0}),
to run a full node, and to contribute to the open-source codebase.
